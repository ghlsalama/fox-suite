# The First 100 Customers — Execution Playbook

## The Math
100 customers × $59/month (bundle) = **$5,900/month MRR**
At 99% margin = $5,841 profit/month

To get 100 paying customers, you need ~1,000 free signups (10% free→paid conversion).
To get 1,000 signups, you need ~20,000 visitors (5% visitor→signup conversion).
To get 20,000 visitors, you need: Product Hunt + outreach + SEO + communities.

---

## Phase 1: First 10 Customers (Week 1-2)

### The "Friends & Family + Free Demo" Strategy
1. **Deploy all 3 products** (4 hours, follow DEPLOYMENT.md)
2. **Create demo links** for each product:
   - ReplyFox: show the bakery demo live
   - PostPilot: show a sample content calendar
   - DescFox: show a before/after Amazon listing
3. **Personally onboard 10 businesses:**
   - Find 10 small businesses you know (local shops, online sellers, friends' businesses)
   - Set up their chatbot/content/descriptions FOR them (free)
   - Show them the results
   - Ask: "Want to keep this? $29/month" or "$59/month for all 3"
   - **Goal: 3-5 convert to paid**

4. **Product Hunt launch** (Day 3-4):
   - Use the demo scripts to record 3 short videos
   - Launch the Fox Suite bundle
   - Email 20 supporters
   - Post in r/SideProject, r/SaaS
   - **Goal: 50-200 free signups from PH traffic**

### Expected results after Week 2:
- 80 free signups
- 5-8 paying customers
- MRR: $150-$470

---

## Phase 2: Next 40 Customers (Week 3-6)

### The "Cold Outreach at Scale" Strategy
1. **Build a prospect list** (200 businesses):
   - Google: "[city] [service]" — find websites with no chatbot
   - Amazon: search for sellers with <4 star listings
   - Instagram: find businesses with <1 post/week
   - Tools: use the gig-hunter scout adapted for prospecting

2. **Daily outreach routine** (1 hour/day):
   - Send 10 personalized emails/day (use templates in OUTREACH.md)
   - Send 5 LinkedIn DMs/day
   - Post 1 genuine helpful comment in r/smallbusiness or r/Shopify
   - **5 days/week × 10 emails = 50/week × 4 weeks = 200 emails**

3. **Response handling:**
   - Anyone who replies → offer a FREE demo (set it up for them)
   - Anyone who signs up free → email them on Day 3: "How's it going?"
   - Anyone who hits the free limit → upgrade prompt + personal email

4. **Cross-sell engine:**
   - Every ReplyFox customer → email about PostPilot (Day 7)
   - Every PostPilot customer → email about DescFox (Day 7)
   - Every DescFox customer → email about ReplyFox (Day 7)
   - Bundle pitch: "Get all 3 for $59 (save $28)"

### Expected results after Week 6:
- 250 free signups (cumulative)
- 30-40 paying customers
- MRR: $1,200-$2,000

## Phase 3: Next 50 Customers (Week 7-12)

### The "Compound Growth" Strategy
1. **SEO content** (automated by fleet blog):
   - Fleet blog writes daily articles about AI for business
   - Topics: "AI chatbot benefits," "social media automation," "Amazon listing tips"
   - Each article links to the Fox Suite
   - Builds organic traffic over months

2. **Affiliate program** (launch in Week 6):
   - 30% recurring commission
   - Recruit from: Reddit communities, Twitter, affiliate networks
   - Provide: demo links, banners, email swipes
   - 10 active affiliates × 5 sales each = 50 customers

3. **Shopify App Store** (launch in Week 8):
   - List ReplyFox as a Shopify app
   - Shopify's marketplace drives organic installs
   - 10M+ Shopify stores = massive distribution

4. **Case studies** (publish weekly):
   - "How [Business] reduced support emails by 80% with ReplyFox"
   - "How [Store] doubled their conversion rate with DescFox"
   - Post on blog + social + communities

5. **Paid acquisition** (start when MRR > $2,000):
   - Google Ads: "AI chatbot for website" ($2-5 CPC)
   - Budget: 20% of MRR = $400/month ad spend
   - Target CPA: $30 (break-even at ~1 month of subscription)

### Expected results after Week 12:
- 600 free signups (cumulative)
- 80-120 paying customers
- MRR: $3,500-$6,000

## The Daily Routine (once deployed)

**Morning (30 min):**
- Check the ops dashboard (MRR, signups, messages)
- Reply to any customer support emails
- Check free-tier users approaching their limit → send upgrade email

**Afternoon (30 min):**
- Send 10 cold outreach emails
- Post 1 piece of content (social media, Reddit, or blog comment)
- Check affiliate signups

**Weekly (2 hours):**
- Write 1 case study or blog post
- Analyze metrics (conversion rate, churn, ARPU)
- Optimize: which product converts best? Double down.

**Total time: 1.5 hours/day.** The products run themselves. You're just selling + optimizing.

## Key Numbers to Track

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → signup rate | 5%+ | Landing page effectiveness |
| Signup → paid rate | 10%+ | Product value + pricing |
| Cross-sell rate | 30%+ | Suite strategy working |
| Monthly churn | <5% | Product stickiness |
| ARPU | $40+ | Upsell effectiveness |
| CAC (customer acquisition cost) | <$30 | Profitable acquisition |
| LTV (lifetime value) | >$300 | 10+ months × $30+ ARPU |

## What NOT to Do

1. **Don't build more features before getting customers.** Deploy what you have. Customer feedback > your assumptions.
2. **Don't compete on price.** $29/$59 is already disruptive. Compete on speed + simplicity + the bundle.
3. **Don't ignore free users.** They're your pipeline. Email them, help them, convert them.
4. **Don't spread too thin.** Focus on ONE product for outreach (the one that converts best), then cross-sell.
5. **Don't give up in Month 1.** SaaS takes 3-6 months to gain traction. The first $1,000 MRR is the hardest.
