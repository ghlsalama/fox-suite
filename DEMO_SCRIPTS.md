## Recording Tips

- Use **OBS Studio** (free) or **Loom** (free tier) for screen recording
- Record at **1920x1080** minimum
- Keep each video under 60 seconds (Product Hunt attention span)
- Add captions/subtitles (many watch without sound)
- Use the demo mode (`npm run demo`) for each product — no real data needed
- Upload to YouTube unlisted → embed on Product Hunt
