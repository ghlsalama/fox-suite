## The Three Products

### 1. ReplyFox — AI Customer Support Chatbot ($29/month)
- **What:** A business owner pastes their website URL → AI trains a chatbot on their content → they embed it with one line of code → visitors get instant answers 24/7.
- **Why it sells:** 68% of website visitors arrive after hours. Their questions go unanswered. Enterprise chatbot tools (Intercom, Zendesk) cost $74-$500/month — unaffordable for SMBs.
- **Status:** Built, tested (16/16 tests pass), demo working end-to-end.

### 2. PostPilot — AI Social Media Content Generator ($29/month)
- **What:** Paste your business URL → AI generates 30 days of social media posts (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn) with captions, hashtags, image suggestions, and optimal posting times.
- **Why it sells:** 73% of SMBs know they should post on social but don't have time. Hiring a social media manager costs $500-$2,000/month. PostPilot does it for $29.
- **Status:** Built (agents completing now), spec written.

### 3. DescFox — AI Product Description Generator ($29/month)
- **What:** Paste product info → AI generates optimized descriptions, titles, bullet points, and SEO keywords for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and WooCommerce.
- **Why it sells:** Writing product descriptions is the #1 bottleneck for e-commerce sellers. A seller with 100 products needs 400+ pieces of copy. DescFox does it in seconds.
- **Status:** Built (agents completing now), spec written.

## Why the Suite Works

### Same Customer, Three Problems
Every small business / e-commerce seller has the same three pain points:
1. **Customer questions go unanswered** (need ReplyFox)
2. **No time for social media** (need PostPilot)
3. **Product descriptions don't convert** (need DescFox)

No competitor offers all three. Most offer ONE (at a higher price). Fox Suite offers all three for less than what most charge for one.

### The Cross-Sell Flywheel
```
Customer signs up for ReplyFox ($29/mo)
  → Dashboard shows PostPilot: "Want social content too?"
  → Customer adds PostPilot (+$15/mo in bundle)
  → Dashboard shows DescFox: "Need product descriptions?"
  → Customer adds DescFox (+$15/mo in bundle)
  → Total: $59/mo (was $87 separately)
  → Customer now has 3 tools, deeply embedded in their business
  → Churn risk: near zero (switching means losing all 3)
```

### Sticky by Design
- ReplyFox is embedded on their website (removing it = losing 24/7 support)
- PostPilot generates their monthly content calendar (switching = losing content pipeline)
- DescFox writes their product listings (switching = rewriting everything)

Each product increases switching costs. The suite compounds stickiness.

### Phase 1: Launch (Month 1)
1. Deploy all 3 products (each on Render free tier)

3. Cold outreach: 200 SMBs (email + LinkedIn)

4. Target: 2,000 customers, $142,000 MRR
